## Description draft

Zielonki XR is being developed to help future residents review a proposed home before construction. The planned experience combines a model overview with human-scale room viewpoints, a design comparison and saved decision notes. Its first scenario focuses on the ground floor and a short seated review.

The project builds on our existing House_Web_MCP architecture editor. We retain its semantic geometry and the Zielonki house model, then develop a dedicated interaction flow for Meta VR devices. The current repository contains the desktop baseline and competition setup. Hand navigation, immersive comparison and the note workflow are not yet complete.
